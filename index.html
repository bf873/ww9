Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to sell their life insurance policy?

+

Generally, policyholders age 65 or older with a policy face value of $100,000 or more may qualify. Recent medical changes, rising premiums, or changes in financial circumstances can also make you eligible. Every situation is reviewed individually by our licensed specialists.

Is the payout taxable?

+

Tax treatment varies by individual circumstances. In many cases, a portion of the settlement may be tax-free, while other portions may be subject to capital gains or ordinary income tax. We recommend consulting with a tax professional. Our advisors can help you understand the general framework.

How long does the process take?

+

Most cases are completed within 30 to 90 days from initial submission to payout. The timeline depends on factors such as policy type, medical records retrieval, and carrier processing times. We keep you informed at every stage.

Is my information kept confidential?

+

Absolutely. All information you share is protected by strict privacy protocols, including 256-bit encryption and HIPAA-compliant data handling standards. We never share your information without your explicit written consent.

Can I sell a term life insurance policy?

+

Yes. Term life policies with conversion privileges may qualify for a life settlement. Whole life, universal life, and group policies may also be eligible. Our specialists can review your specific policy to determine what options are available.

Is there any obligation or cost to check my eligibility?

+

None whatsoever. The eligibility check and policy review are completely free and carry zero obligation. You can choose to stop the process at any point. We believe in providing information so you can make the best decision for your family.
